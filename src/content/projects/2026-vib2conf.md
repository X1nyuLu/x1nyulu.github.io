Vib2Conf connects subtle differences in vibrational spectra with molecular conformations, moving spectrum interpretation beyond molecular identity toward three-dimensional structural discrimination.

![Vib2Conf graphical abstract showing its spectral encoder, molecular encoder, attentional resampler, and mixture-of-experts architecture.](/img/projects/vib2conf-toc.png)

Published in **Analytical Chemistry** on August 25, 2026. The [journal article](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.analchem.6c02845) and [arXiv preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.24310) are available online.

**My role:** method development, model training, spectral analysis, and experimental validation.
